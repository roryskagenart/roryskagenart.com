01 / Brand vision

Make Rory the visual signature of Austin’s weird optimism.

Make Rory’s work part of how Austin recognizes itself—and how the rest of the world recognizes Austin.

Rory has the ingredients of a lasting cultural brand: four decades of visual work, the Greetings from Austin mural, co-founder credibility, and a top-five seller reputation at Blue Genie Art Bazaar. Turn that trust into a living portfolio: original paintings and collector editions at the top, accessible art and considered objects below, and a steady stream of stories that make each piece mean more.

Brand promise Real Austin provenance. Absurd optimism. Art and objects that say: “I know this city—and I know the artist who made it.”

A simple year of milestones: get ready for the holiday Bazaar, welcome new collectors, use the spring market to grow the audience, then turn the best stories into year-round direct sales.

THE BAZAAR STAYS A SELLING MOMENT

Direct-to-consumer (DTC) adds a year-round connection; it does not replace or undercut Blue Genie. Keep the holiday and spring market as important in-person sales opportunities. Invite visitors to follow Rory, join the email list, or read the story if they choose; use those relationships for future releases, restocks, and direct purchases after the event.
